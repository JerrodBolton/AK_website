About the Kingdom

A Kingdom Built on Connection

Animai Kingdom is a student-led exploration of how AI can feel more human. Instead of talking to a blank screen, users interact with unique animal companions that have their own personalities and purposes.

The project focuses on the bridge between the digital and physical world. By using 3D prototyping and hardware like Raspberry Pi, Animai Kingdom is working toward a future where these companions can sit on a desk and assist users in the real world.

We do not just build chatbots. We build characters with heart, designed to help users stay motivated, organized, calm, and creative.

Meet the Companions

The Five Guardians of Animai Kingdom

Click any companion card to discover their full story, purpose, and future AI role.

HeyShark

🌊 Tideforge Harbor

Direct · High-Energy · Action-Focused

The Mobility Base

HeyShark uses a wheelchair-style mobility base. This design represents adaptability and strength — showing that progress is not about how you move, but that you keep moving forward. It also serves as the foundation for the physical desk-bot prototype.

Role & Future

He acts as an accountability partner. In the future, he will track goals and push users to stay disciplined.

"Stop waiting for the perfect tide. Start swimming now."

Penguin

❄️ Frostbyte Village

Loyal · Steady · Cheerful

The Jacket

Even in the cold, Penguin wears custom-made jackets representing his village's love for craftsmanship and being prepared. Practically, this allows the project to test complex 3D printing textures and fabric details on physical models.

Role & Future

He represents comfort and steady growth. Future AI versions will help users celebrate small wins and daily progress.

"Small steps still get you where you're going — especially if you're cozy!"

Hank the Eagle

🦅 Skyreach Court

Wise · Professional · Observant

The Professional Look

Hank is always dressed in polished, regal attire. Because he flies high and sees the big picture, his appearance reflects leadership, honor, and high standards befitting a royal advisor.

Role & Future

He helps users plan and organize. Future AI features include summarizing complex information and providing strategic advice.

"Perspective is everything. Let's look at the big picture together."

Monkey King

🌄 Suncrest Peaks

Playful · Bold · Energetic

The Humor

The Monkey King is always joking — using humor to break tension and build confidence, turning every challenge into a fun adventure. His jokes are not a sign of being unserious; they are a sign of his cleverness and creative spirit.

Role & Future

He helps unlock imagination. Future AI use involves creative brainstorming and gamifying difficult tasks.

"If it's not an adventure, you're probably doing it wrong!"

Panda

🎋 Bamboo Haven

Grounded · Peaceful · Supportive

The Farmer's Calm

Panda's peaceful nature comes from his background as a farmer. Tending the land taught him that everything has its own time and rhythm — and he brings that patience to every interaction.

Role & Future

He acts as a grounding force for emotional support. Future versions will focus on wellness, mindfulness, and work-life balance.

"Breathe. Even the tallest bamboo grows one patient inch at a time."
