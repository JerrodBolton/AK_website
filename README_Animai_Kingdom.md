# Animai Kingdom Website

## Project Overview

**Animai Kingdom** is a Forest Daybreak Fantasy AI website about animal-based AI companions. The website introduces five companions, each with a unique personality, purpose, design explanation, and future AI use.

The project combines fantasy storytelling, artificial intelligence, 3D modeling, voice assistant development, physical prototyping, and future Raspberry Pi hardware integration.

This website was created for a class assignment where the goal was to design a useful app or website with the help of multiple AI employees. The website is not only a demo that AI can create a website. It serves as the official home for the Animai Kingdom project, helping visitors understand the vision, meet the companions, view progress, and see future development plans.

---

## Website Theme

**Theme Name:** Forest Daybreak Fantasy AI

The design is inspired by a magical forest kingdom at sunrise. The website uses warm green, orange, and gold colors to create a welcoming fantasy feeling while still including subtle futuristic AI elements.

### Color Palette

| Color | Hex Code | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Deep Forest Green | `#123D27` | Header, footer, dark sections |
| Leaf Green | `#3F8A3E` | Section accents and cards |
| Bright Kingdom Green | `#85C441` | Highlights and hover effects |
| Sunrise Orange | `#F58220` | Buttons and call-to-action areas |
| Golden Yellow | `#F7C948` | Fantasy accents and details |
| Soft Cream | `#FFF4DF` | Background and readable content areas |
| Dark Moss | `#16251B` | Text and contrast |

## Website Sections

The website is built as a one-page scrolling website with the following sections:

1. **Home / Enter the Kingdom**  
   Introduces the project, logo, theme, and main purpose.

2. **About the Kingdom**  
   Explains why Animai Kingdom was created and how it connects AI, storytelling, 3D modeling, and future physical prototypes.

3. **Meet the Companions**  
   Introduces the five main animal AI companions.

4. **From Concept to Companion / Project Progress**  
   Shows the progress of the project from character ideas to prototype planning.

5. **AI Employees**  
   Explains how different AI tools helped create the project.

6. **How to Use the Website**  
   Gives visitors simple instructions for exploring the website.

7. **Join the Kingdom / Contact / Future Work**  
   Shows future project goals and includes a simple contact form.

## Companions

### HeyShark | The Motivation Coach

- **Realm:** Tideforge Harbor  
- **Personality:** Direct, high-energy, and action-focused  
- **Role:** Accountability and motivation companion  
- **Design Detail:** HeyShark uses a wheelchair-style mobility base to represent adaptability, strength, accessibility, and the physical desk-bot prototype direction of the project.  
- **Future AI Use:** Goal tracking, motivation, reminders, and Raspberry Pi desk-bot development  

### Penguin | The Cozy Prototype

- **Realm:** Frostbyte Village  
- **Personality:** Loyal, steady, and cheerful  
- **Role:** Comfort and steady progress companion  
- **Design Detail:** Penguin wears jackets to represent craftsmanship, preparation, and 3D printing texture testing.  
- **Future AI Use:** Progress tracking, small-win celebrations, and prototype testing  

### Hank the Eagle | The Royal Advisor

- **Realm:** Skyreach Court  
- **Personality:** Wise, professional, and observant  
- **Role:** Planning and strategy companion  
- **Design Detail:** Hank dresses professionally because he represents leadership, honor, high standards, and big-picture thinking.  
- **Future AI Use:** Planning support, summarization, strategic advice, and organization  

### Monkey King | The Creative Adventurer

- **Realm:** Suncrest Peaks  
- **Personality:** Playful, bold, and energetic  
- **Role:** Creativity and brainstorming companion  
- **Design Detail:** Monkey King uses humor to break tension, build confidence, and turn challenges into adventures.  
- **Future AI Use:** Creative brainstorming, idea generation, and gamified task support  

### Panda | The Calm Guide

- **Realm:** Bamboo Haven  
- **Personality:** Grounded, peaceful, and supportive  
- **Role:** Wellness and emotional support companion  
- **Design Detail:** Panda’s calm personality comes from his farmer background, where patience, rhythm, and balance are part of daily life.  
- **Future AI Use:** Mindfulness, wellness support, breathing exercises, and work-life balance reminders

## Assignment Purpose

This website was created for an assignment requiring students to choose an app or website with a clear and useful purpose, use multiple AI employees to develop the software, ensure the code is well-commented, and create a project that is innovative, intuitive, and easy to use.

Animai Kingdom meets these requirements by serving as a real project website for an ongoing AI companion concept. It introduces the project vision, explains the animal companions, documents development progress, and shows how AI tools can work together as a creative development team.
